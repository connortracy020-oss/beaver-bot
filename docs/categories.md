# Categories

The 12 task-based categories from [structure-map.md](structure-map.md) ("Getting in" and the campus-life scope of "Housing, dining & campus life" added with Tier 2b), with an example question, the main official sources, and the office to send a student to when we can't answer.

- **How pages are assigned:** `python -m assistant.categories` gives every page a primary category from its site and section, plus secondary ones from title keywords. The results go in the `page_categories` table.
- **Sources are OSU's own:** each source below is a crawled OSU page, shown with its owning office. Every answer must link to one.

## Pages with no clear owner

Every stored page has an owning *site*. But for the questions students ask, three groups have no clear owner:

### A. Topics split across offices

The answer has to combine pages, and the app should name every office involved.

| Topic | Sites | Why it's split |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition refunds after dropping or withdrawing | registrar, finance, financialaid | The refund deadlines are on the Registrar's academic calendar; the money side (bills, credits) is Student Accounts; aid adjustments are Financial Aid. No single page owns the whole answer. |
| Registration holds | holds, registrar, finance | Holds @ OSU explains each hold type, but each hold is cleared by a different office (Student Accounts, Student Health, Registrar, advising). |
| Food insecurity and meal help | basicneeds, uhds, studentlife, food | Basic Needs Center, UHDS (meal plans, food insecurity pages) and Food @ OSU all cover parts; the older HSRC pages on studentlife are unpublished (Access denied). |
| Services for online students | ecampus, ds, advising, career, library | Ecampus describes DAS, advising, career and library services for online students, but the service itself belongs to the main office; answers should cite both. |
| Exam schedules and conflicts | registrar, ecampus | The Registrar owns final exam policy; Ecampus owns proctoring for online exams. |
| Transfer credit | admissions, transfer, catalog, coreeducation | Admissions publishes the course-by-course tables for each community college; Transfer Students advises; the catalog and Core Education say how credit counts toward a degree. |
| Veterans' education benefits | veterans, registrar, finance | Military & Veteran Resources explains the GI Bill chapters and has the VA certifying team; the Registrar explains how dropping classes affects benefits; the bill itself is Student Accounts'. |
